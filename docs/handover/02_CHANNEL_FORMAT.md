# Channel format (Iron Within to space)

Working titles: Cradle Is Not Home / The Iron Orbit / Leave the Well

## Runtime

- Target 16-22 minutes
- Hook 0:00-0:45 (must work as a Short later)
- 3-4 chapters
- Close + subscribe CTA last 20s

Chapter titles are accusations, not topics.

## Script voice

- Second person, fragments, one-line punches
- No Wikipedia. Space facts only as pressure.
- Original copy only. Do not imitate named living speakers.

## Picture

- Real NASA mission film first
- Slow zoom, hard cuts on stressed words
- Dark grade, grain, crushed blacks
- Captions only on the hardest lines
- Mute source audio; licensed music ducked under VO

Visuals: NASA public-domain mission footage, edited and graded. NASA does not endorse this channel. Script and narration original. Music licensed.
