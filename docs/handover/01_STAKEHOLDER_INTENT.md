# Stakeholder intent

## Product

A **fully automated** pipeline that creates and uploads **one original long-form motivational YouTube video per day**.

Theme: **spaceflight and humanity leaving Earth** — multi-planetary identity, discipline, cost, isolation, standards, time.

Format/style model: **@ironwithin / @ironwithinofficial / @IronnWithin**

- Long-form 16–22 minutes (primary)
- Confrontational second-person script
- Short punchy lines, chapters
- Cinematic dark grade, captions on hard lines
- Deep intense consistent VO
- Daily cadence

**Visuals: real footage is better than AI video.** NASA public-domain mission film is the backbone. AI clips are optional fill only.

## Goals

1. Zero daily editing once v1 is stable (5-minute kill-switch review allowed for first 30 days).
2. Original scripts — **do not** compile living speakers. Copy **cadence and structure**, not their audio.
3. Monetization-safe: NASA PD + licensed music + original narration + transformative edit.
4. Shorts from the hook are phase 2.

## Non-goals (do not build yet)

- Talking-head avatar
- Scraping gym/fight edits
- Celebrity speech compilations
- Claiming NASA endorsement
- Low-quality volume farm
- Full After Effects parity on day one

## Constraints

- NASA media OK with credit, no meatball-as-logo. Music licensed. No SpaceX brand films as PD.
- YouTube API upload starts **unlisted**.
- Job fails closed if snippet library is too thin.

## Success (90 days)

- 60+ original videos published
- Consistent voice, grade, titles
- No copyright strikes from VO/music
- Stakeholder time under 15 min/day after week 2
